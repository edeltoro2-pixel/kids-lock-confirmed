Privacy policy

Last updated October 28, 2026

Learning Break is an app for children in Pre-K through 5th grade, made by PrintForge Studios ("we", "us"). After a set amount of play time, it pauses the device for a short learning break. This policy explains what information the app and this website use, why, who else handles it, how long we keep it, and how parents can see, change or delete it.

The short version

  • Children never create accounts or type in personal information. Their answers, stars and progress stay on the device.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking. We don't sell or share personal information.
  • A parent account is optional. It holds the parent's email address and password, and for each child a nickname, grade, settings and word list, so they're the same on every device.
  • Parents can delete the account and all of its data at any time, in the app or on the web.

What stays on the device

The app works fully without an account. Everything in this section stays on the phone or tablet. We never receive it, and removing the app deletes it.

  • Settings: the child's grade, subjects, break timing and read-aloud choice, plus your word list.
  • The parent PIN, kept as a salted hash in the device's encrypted storage.
  • Children's answers, stars and progress during breaks.
  • The break timer. To know when a break is due, the app counts how long the screen has been on and unlocked. It doesn't see or record which other apps are used.
  • Read-aloud, which uses the device's own text-to-speech voice.

On Android, the app asks to "display over other apps" so a break can cover the screen, and to show notifications ("Next learning break in 12 min"). It also starts again after the device restarts, so breaks keep working. It doesn't use the camera, microphone, location, contacts, photos or files.

What a parent account stores

If you create a parent account, we store:

  • Your email address, and your password in hashed form. Our sign-in provider stores only the hash, so nobody can read the password, including us.
  • For each child you add: a nickname (a first name or a made-up one; no last name needed), a grade, the app's settings, and the word list you made for that child (words, and any meanings you typed). Automatic breaks stay a setting on each device and aren't stored.
  • When the account was created and last signed in.

We use this only to sign you in, to keep each child's settings and words the same on all your devices, and to send emails about the account, such as the sign-up confirmation. We don't use it for advertising or profiling, and we don't sell it.

When a device signs in or syncs, our hosting provider receives the device's IP address and basic connection details, as with any internet service. These go into short-lived service logs used for security and troubleshooting.

Children's privacy

Learning Break is made for children ages 3 to 11 and their parents. It's designed to follow the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Google Play's Families policy.

  • Children can't create accounts. Only a parent can, in the parent area, which is protected by the parent PIN.
  • We don't ask children for personal information. The app has no ads, chat or sharing, and no links out of the app outside the parent area.
  • A child's nickname, grade and word list are entered by the parent.
  • If we learn that we received personal information from a child without a parent's consent, we delete it.

Parents can:

  • See each child's nickname and grade under Parent settings › Account and sync, and their settings and word list in Parent settings.
  • Change the grade, settings and word list at any time.
  • Stop syncing by signing out, or delete everything by deleting the account.
  • Ask us by email for a copy of their data, or to correct or delete it.

Who else handles the information

We share personal information only with the service providers that run parts of Learning Break for us, and only so they can do that job:

  • Supabase, Inc. hosts the account database and sign-in, and sends account emails. Its servers are in the United States (Amazon Web Services, us-east-1).
  • GitHub, Inc. hosts this website (GitHub Pages). GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security.
  • Google distributes the app through Google Play. Google's own privacy policy covers what Google Play collects.

We'll also disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect the safety of a child or someone else. If PrintForge Studios is ever sold or merged, this policy still applies to information collected under it, and we'll tell parents before anything changes.

This website

This website uses no cookies, analytics or ads, and loads nothing from other companies. On the delete-account page, the email address and password you type go to our sign-in provider to sign you in. Nothing is saved in your browser after you leave the page.

How long we keep it

  • On the device: until you remove the app or clear its data.
  • A parent account: until you delete it. The account and all of its children's data are then deleted from our database straight away. Copies in our hosting provider's backups and logs are gone within 30 days.
  • Service logs with IP addresses: deleted by our hosting provider within 30 days.
  • A sign-up that was never confirmed: email us and we'll delete it.

Deleting your account

Deleting the account removes your email address and password and every child's nickname, grade, settings and word list. It can't be undone.

  • In the app: Parent › enter your PIN › Parent settings › Account and sync › Delete account. Type your password and tap Delete my account.
  • On the web: use the delete-account page in any browser.
  • By email: write to reactforgeai@gmail.com from the account's email address. We'll delete it within 7 days and reply when it's done.

Each device keeps its own settings, PIN and word list until the app is removed from it.

Security

  • Information travels encrypted (HTTPS), and our hosting provider stores it encrypted.
  • Database rules let each parent account reach only its own family's data.
  • On the device, the sign-in is kept in encrypted storage and left out of device backups, and the parent PIN is stored only as a hash.

No system is perfectly secure. If you think something is wrong, please tell us.

Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live (for example California or another US state, the European Union or the United Kingdom), you may have the right to know what information we hold about you, to get a copy, and to correct or delete it. Email us to use these rights; we'll answer within 30 days. We don't sell or share personal information as California law defines those words, and we don't use it for targeted advertising.

In the EU and UK, we use account information to provide the service you asked for, and service logs for our legitimate interest in keeping the service secure. Account data is stored in the United States. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll post the new version here with a new date. If a change affects children's information or how we use your information, we'll tell parents in the app or by email before it takes effect.
